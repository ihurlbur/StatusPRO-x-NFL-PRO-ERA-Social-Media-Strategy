# StatusPRO x NFL PRO ERA Social Media Strategy

Social media growth strategy developed for StatusPRO and NFL PRO ERA as part of a team-based consulting project at USC Marshall. The project examines how StatusPRO can expand awareness, attract new users, and drive game sales by strengthening NFL PRO ERA’s social media presence.

## Overview

NFL PRO ERA offers football fans and virtual reality gamers an immersive, first-person experience that allows users to step onto the field as an NFL player.

This project evaluates StatusPRO’s existing social media activity and develops a strategy to reach a broader audience of football fans, gamers, and younger consumers. The analysis focuses on how StatusPRO can increase engagement while working within the constraints of a small social media team.

## Objectives

- Evaluate the current social media presence of StatusPRO and NFL PRO ERA
- Identify opportunities to reach casual football and gaming audiences
- Benchmark posting strategies used by sports gaming and VR competitors
- Develop platform-specific content and account strategies
- Recommend partnerships with the NFL, athletes, and digital creators
- Establish an implementation plan and performance metrics

## Research and Analysis

The project included:

- Analysis of StatusPRO and NFL PRO ERA content across Instagram, YouTube, and X
- Competitive benchmarking against EA Sports FC, Madden, and other VR sports games
- Research into the social media, sports, and gaming habits of Gen Z consumers
- Evaluation of user-generated content, influencer marketing, and community-building opportunities
- Segmentation of the StatusPRO corporate brand and NFL PRO ERA game accounts

## Strategic Recommendations

The final strategy centered on four areas:

1. **Increase Posting Frequency**  
   Establish a consistent schedule of three to four weekly posts in the short term, with a long-term goal of daily content.

2. **Expand Content Diversity**  
   Incorporate gameplay highlights, user-generated content, tutorials, interactive posts, behind-the-scenes features, and NFL calendar-based content.

3. **Differentiate Platforms and Accounts**  
   Position StatusPRO accounts around company news, partnerships, technology, and thought leadership while focusing NFL PRO ERA accounts on gameplay, competitions, updates, and community engagement.

4. **Develop Strategic Partnerships**  
   Collaborate with the NFL, Meta, NFL players, and sports gaming creators to expand reach and reduce the internal resources required to produce new content.

## Project Context

This project was completed by a team of seven USC Marshall MBA students with professional experience across consulting, project management, finance, investment banking, gaming, and the military.

The recommendations were developed as a strategic consulting exercise based on market research, social media analysis, competitor benchmarking, and audience insights. The project does not represent confirmation that StatusPRO implemented any of the proposed strategies.
